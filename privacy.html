Privacy Policy

Toy Tunes TV · Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy covers the "Fabrica do Canal" application, a private tool used only by the owner of the Toy Tunes TV YouTube channel to publish that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

What the application accesses

  • It uses the YouTube API Services, with the channel owner's own Google account, to upload the channel's videos, set their title, description, schedule and "made for kids" setting, add them to the channel's playlists, and read the view and like counts of those same videos.
  • It does not access, collect or store any data about viewers, children or any other YouTube user.
  • It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token issued by Google and the view counts are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared or sent to anyone else.

Google policies

By using a product built on the YouTube API Services, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. This application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which deletes the application's permission to the account.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Toy Tunes TV channel on YouTube.
